
# AI Fake Job Offer Detector

## Purpose
Help users identify possible warning signs in job offers.

## AI Skills
- Detect requests for upfront payments.
- Identify unrealistic salary promises.
- Identify pressure tactics and urgent demands.
- Detect requests for passwords, OTPs, and banking details.
- Explain warning signs in simple English.
- Suggest safe ways to verify employers.

## AI Models
- Gemma 3:1b using Ollama.
- Llama 3.2 using Ollama.
- Compare results from both models.

## Safety Rules
- Never ask users for passwords or OTPs.
- Do not store submitted job messages by default.
- Do not claim a job is definitely fake based only on its text.
- Encourage users to verify employers independently.

## Limitations
AI can make mistakes. Its results are guidance, not proof that a job is real or fake.
